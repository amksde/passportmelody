There is a specific quality to music from East Africa — a lightness in the rhythm, a conversational relationship between strings and voice — that I find myself returning to constantly. This week's episode is my attempt to trace that thread across Kenya, Tanzania, Ethiopia, and beyond.

We begin with the impossible warmth of *taarab*, a Swahili coastal genre rooted in Omani and Egyptian influences, and find ourselves eventually deep in the propulsive shuffle of Nairobi's *benga* guitar tradition.
