The Iberian peninsula has two great traditions of sorrow set to string. Portugal has *fado* — fate, literally, sung into a void. Spain has *flamenco* — duende, a spirit summoned from the earth through heel and voice. This week I tried to sit with both.

There is no competition between them. They share a Moorish ancestry and an understanding that music can hold grief without resolving it. I hope this episode captures some of that.
