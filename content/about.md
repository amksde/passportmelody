Hello. I'm the person behind Passport Melody.

Every week, I sit down and pick ten pieces of music that have moved me — songs from corners of the world I've visited through headphones, records, and the odd well-worn vinyl. The selections are personal. They follow no genre rule, no geography quota. Just music that deserved a moment of your time.

Passport Melody began as a notebook I kept for myself. Playlists with little notes in the margins. At some point it felt selfish not to share it.

Each episode is a postcard. The songs are the stamps.

If something here takes you somewhere unexpected, that's the whole idea.
